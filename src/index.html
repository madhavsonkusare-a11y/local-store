A home for self-hosted apps.
Discover independent software, install reviewed recipes, and connect apps you already run.
Install previews. Memos, n8n, and Uptime Kuma have pinned recipes awaiting full real-container verification. Local Store shows each container, port, storage, and risks before installation.
No Local Store account. Connections and managed app records are saved on this computer. Apps retain their own sign-in requirements.
Open source. Built with Tauri, Inter, and Lucide. Catalog information combines awesome-selfhosted, Runtipi, CasaOS/ZimaOS and Coolify. Each app links to its sources.
